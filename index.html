The planning question

Which venue should an event team shortlist when it needs to fit an expected audience while keeping the trip reasonable for transit riders? This tool ranks a transparent, small comparison set. It does not replace venue availability, quotes, permits, or an on-site visit.

Data and method

Sources, assumptions, and uncertainty

How we collected it

The supplied CSV records eight selected venues using venue-owner or public-project materials. Each row includes source URLs and a recorded access date of 17 September 2026. We retain those observations without claiming a fresh source audit. Capacities refer to different concert, reception, or seated configurations. Distance values are supplied approximate straight-line estimates: coordinates, stop IDs, and calculation steps were not provided, so we cannot reproduce or verify them. MBTA GTFS documentation is background context, not evidence that these distances were calculated from GTFS. Boston Landing is a commuter-rail stop; the listed stops are not a uniform rapid-transit comparison.

Missing data and limits

Capacities depend on layout, seating, production, and fire-code approvals. The tool does not measure current availability, rental cost, accessibility features, neighborhood impacts, actual walking routes, crowd demographics, ticket demand, or guest satisfaction. Transit distance is not travel time and does not represent service frequency or disruptions. The eight-venue set is selective, so it supports a first shortlist rather than a market-wide conclusion. Zero parking entries are unverified and must not be read as confirmed absence of parking. Source access dates do not establish current availability or unchanged capacities.

How candidates are ordered

First, we keep venues whose recorded capacity can hold your audience and that match your selected setting, event season, and event seating. Spring, Autumn, and Winter exclude summer-only venues. Summer and “Not decided” include them, with their seasonal status shown.

“Any arrangement” includes all recorded capacity types, which may refer to seated, standing, or reception layouts. “Documented seated capacity only” includes venues whose dataset notes specifically describe a seated capacity. Other venues may offer seating, but their seated capacity isn’t specified in this dataset.

Matching venues appear from the smallest sufficient recorded capacity to the largest. Ties are sorted alphabetically by venue name. The numbers beside candidates show their position in this list, not a quality score. A venue whose recorded capacity is more than twice your audience is labelled “Much larger than audience”. This comparison does not establish the venue’s minimum booking size.

The optional distance filter uses unverified straight-line estimates and can remove candidates, but it does not change their order. The revenue illustration is attendance × $50, before costs; it does not predict ticket sales.
