The challenge
Design and build a useful Site based on data you collect or obtain from reliable public sources. Your Site must help a business, government agency, nonprofit, or community group understand a problem and make a decision.
Choose one of the challenge areas below—or propose a similar area with your instructor’s approval.
Challenge areas.
Public transport improvement
Identify routes, stops, or times where transport services could improve. Use ridership, delays, journey times, service frequency, accessibility, or student survey data.
Community energy dashboard
Help a city or business understand energy costs and sources. Compare consumption, prices, solar and wind production, emissions, imports, and opportunities to reduce costs.
Small-business market opportunity
Identify an unmet customer need or promising product category. Collect survey data and examine prices, customer preferences, competitors, demand, and purchasing habits.
Road safety priorities
Help a local authority decide where safety improvements are most urgent. Analyze crashes, traffic volumes, speeding, lighting, crossings, road conditions, and public reports.
Tourism planning
Help a tourism organization attract visitors while managing local impacts. Compare visitor numbers, seasonal demand, attractions, accommodation, spending, transport, and resident feedback.
Public health resource planning
Help a health agency identify where services or information are most needed. Use non-personal data on population, service access, waiting times, facilities, health indicators, or community surveys.
School facilities and student experience
Help school leaders improve facilities or services. Collect anonymous data about study spaces, food services, technology, transport, extracurricular activities, or student wellbeing.
Emergency service coverage
Help emergency planners identify areas with slower access to fire, ambulance, or rescue services. Examine response times, station locations, population, road access, incident volumes, and service demand.
Workforce skills planning
Help employers or government agencies identify gaps between available skills and local job opportunities. Compare job advertisements, required qualifications, salaries, training courses, and student or worker surveys.
Government budget transparency
Build a Site that helps residents understand how a public budget is allocated. Compare spending by department, service, location, population group, project, and year.
Event and venue planning
Help a business, university, or government agency plan events more effectively. Analyze attendance, ticket prices, venue capacity, schedules, audience preferences, travel times, and satisfaction ratings.
Food-service improvement
Help a school, hospital, workplace, or restaurant improve its food services. Examine prices, waiting times, menu choices, dietary requirements, customer satisfaction, demand by time, and food availability.
Site requirements
You should create a folder on your local machine and select it for a Codex Project. 
Your Site should include:
A clear statement of the problem and intended user
An explanation of how the data was collected
At least three useful charts, maps, tables, or indicators
Filters or comparison controls that let users explore or compare the data
A summary of the most important findings
Two or more practical recommendations supported by the data
URL links to Sources, dates, units, and definitions
A discussion of missing data, bias, uncertainty, and limitations
A design that works on both computers and phones
Do not collect names, contact details, medical information, precise personal locations, or other sensitive data.
Deliverables
Each team will submit:
The published Site
The collected dataset in CSV or spreadsheet format
A one-page data and methodology note
A five-minute presentation demonstrating the Site
A short reflection explaining what the data supports—and what it cannot prove
