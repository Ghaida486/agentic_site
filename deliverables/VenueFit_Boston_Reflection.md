# VenueFit Boston Reflection

VenueFit supports an initial venue shortlist for Boston event planners. The data shows that venues offer distinct tradeoffs in capacity, setting, and rapid-transit proximity. For example, the 3,500-person Roadrunner and Wang Theatre are closer to rapid transit than the 5,000-person Leader Bank Pavilion, while the Pavilion offers the larger outdoor summer option.

The site cannot prove that a venue will produce better attendance, satisfaction, revenue, or operational outcomes. It does not include current availability, actual rental quotes, walking routes, service frequency, weather, production needs, or attendee preferences. The transit-distance measure is a straight-line proxy, not a travel-time estimate. Capacity is also configuration-specific and may not match a particular concert, lecture, gala, or conference.

The tool should therefore support a first decision, not make the final booking decision. A planner should contact shortlisted venues, confirm the desired configuration and accessibility provisions, check the event date against service and weather conditions, and compare quotes before contracting.
